Public computers

Members get 90 minutes per day. Non-members may book a 30-minute guest session with photo ID. Printing is 10p per side, colour 40p. Filtering is on for under-16 tickets.

No booking yet.

Loans, holds, and fines

The standard loan is three weeks and renews twice unless another member is waiting. A hold costs nothing. Items not collected within seven days of arrival are released to the next person on the list.

Overdue fines are 20p per item per day after a one-day grace, capped at £8 per item. Children’s tickets do not accrue fines; lost items are still charged at replacement cost.

Digital magazines through PressReader do not count against the physical loan cap of 12 items.

Joining

Bring one proof of address dated within three months and photo ID. Children under 16 need a parent or guardian. Non-residents who work in the city may join with a payslip. There is no joining fee.

Catalogue services

Search the catalogue from any branch, place up to eight holds, and choose a collection branch. Audio and large-print titles can be delivered to a branch twice a week.

Reserve an item

Holds are free. You have seven days after an arrival notice to collect.

Digital library

Borrow ebooks, audiobooks, magazines, and language courses with your library number and PIN.

Home delivery

Residents who cannot visit because of disability or temporary illness can request monthly delivery.

Learning and support

  • Weekly job club offers CV reviews, interview practice, and basic digital-skills help.
  • Digital helpers can assist with phone settings, online forms, and avoiding scams; they cannot give legal or financial advice.
  • ESOL conversation groups are free and do not require a library membership.
  • Schools may book class visits four weeks ahead through the Riverside learning team.
